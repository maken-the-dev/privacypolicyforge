At maken.dev, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how BattleCall ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

🔒 Key Principle: Everything personal — your goals, alarms, recordings and streak — stays on your device. maken.dev operates no servers and never receives that data. The only things that leave your device are anonymous usage analytics, ads (free users), purchase verification, and (if you choose AI Coach) the text used to write your wake-up message.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use BattleCall, you may provide the following information, which is stored locally on your device only:

  • Personal Goals: Your name, life goals, motivational statements, and daily plans
  • Alarm Data: Alarm times, labels, categories, and unlock method preferences
  • Audio Recordings: Voice memos you record for custom alarm sounds (stored locally)
  • Media Files: Custom audio or video files you choose as alarm sounds
  • QR Code Data: QR codes you generate for alarm dismissal challenges
  • Object-Scan Photos: A photo of an object you choose so the alarm can be dismissed by scanning that same object. Stored only on this device and never uploaded
  • Usage Statistics: Your alarm completion streak and history (stored locally)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

The app automatically collects the following technical information:

  • App Installation Date: To track your free trial period (stored locally)
  • Device State: To ensure alarms function properly (wake locks, audio state)
  • Analytics Events: Which screens you open and which options you pick — alarm type, sound mode, ringtone, unlock method, and purchase events. These never include your name, goals, alarm labels, voice recordings, or QR code contents
  • Device & App Info: Device model, Android version, language, country, and app version, recorded alongside analytics events
  • Advertising ID: Your device's resettable Android advertising identifier, used to measure our advertising campaigns
  • Crash Logs: Anonymous diagnostic data to improve app stability

2. Permissions We Request

🔔
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Required to show alarm notifications and remind you of scheduled alarms.
⏰
Schedule Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) Required to trigger alarms at precise times you set. This permission is essential for alarm functionality.
🖥️
Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Required to show the ringing screen on top of whatever app is open when an alarm fires.
📺
Full-Screen Notifications (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) Required to open the alarm full-screen on your lock screen instead of as a small notification.
🔊
Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) Required to keep the alarm sound playing reliably for as long as the alarm is ringing.
🎤
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Only used if you choose to record custom voice memos for alarm sounds. Recordings are stored locally on your device.
📷
Camera (CAMERA) Used if you choose QR scanning or object-scan unlock. Object-scan photos stay on this device and are never uploaded. The flashlight is used only while you are capturing or matching, if you turn it on.
🚶
Physical Activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) Only used if you choose the step-count unlock mission. Steps are counted while the alarm rings and are never stored or transmitted.
📂
Storage Access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Used to save and access custom alarm sounds and QR code images you choose to export.
🔋
Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK) Required to wake your device and keep the screen on when an alarm rings.
🔌
Ignore Battery Optimisation (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS) Optional. Asks Android not to put BattleCall to sleep, so scheduled alarms are not silently skipped.
🔄
Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Required to restore your scheduled alarms after device restart.
🎵
Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) Required to set alarm volume to maximum to ensure you hear your alarm (Ultra Volume).
📳
Vibrate (VIBRATE) Required to vibrate the device while an alarm is ringing.
🌐
Internet (INTERNET) Required for AI Coach generation, purchase validation, anonymous analytics, and ads.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used on your device for the following purposes:

  • Alarm Functionality: To schedule, trigger, and manage your alarms and reminders
  • AI Coach Feature: To generate personalized motivational messages using your goals and preferences
  • User Experience: To remember your preferences, track your streak, and provide a personalized experience

Anonymous, non-personal information is used for:

  • Product Decisions: Aggregated analytics tell us which features are actually used
  • Ad Measurement: Your advertising ID lets Google attribute app installs to our own campaigns
  • App Improvements: Anonymous crash data is collected to fix bugs and improve performance

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Gemini AI

BattleCall uses Google Gemini AI to generate personalized AI coach messages and text-to-speech audio. When you use the AI Coach feature:

  • Your name, goals, and daily plans are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate motivational content
  • This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS
  • Google may process this data according to their Privacy Policy
  • You can opt out of AI Coach features and use traditional alarm sounds instead
⚠️ Important: When using AI Coach features, your goals and daily plans are temporarily sent to Google's servers for AI processing. If you prefer complete privacy, use the Ringtone, Voice Memo, or Custom Media sound modes instead.

4.2 Firebase Analytics & Advertising ID

BattleCall uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to measure our advertising:

  • Anonymous events are logged: screens opened, alarms created and completed, and which options you chose (alarm type, sound mode, ringtone, unlock method, purchases)
  • Device model, Android version, language, country, and app version are recorded alongside those events
  • Your device's Android advertising ID is collected so Google can attribute app installs to our campaigns and build audience segments
  • Your name, goals, alarm labels, voice recordings, and QR code contents are never included
  • Google processes this data according to their Privacy Policy
🎯 Ad Tracking Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops advertising-based measurement for this app.

4.3 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

BattleCall uses RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions and in-app purchases. When you make a purchase:

  • Your anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt are shared with RevenueCat to verify your entitlements
  • RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or other personally identifiable information
  • All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play — we never have access to your card details
  • RevenueCat processes data according to their Privacy Policy

4.4 Google Play In-App Review

After a few successful alarms, BattleCall may ask whether you'd like to rate the app. If you agree, Google Play's own review sheet opens inside the app.

  • Any review you write is submitted to and stored by Google Play, not by us
  • Google never tells us whether you left a review or what it said
  • Only your answer to our own question ("Rate", "Maybe later", "Don't ask again") is saved locally, so we know not to keep asking

4.5 Google AdMob

BattleCall shows full-screen ads to free users a few seconds after creating a regular alarm and after completing an alarm challenge. Ads are served by Google AdMob:

  • Your advertising ID, approximate location, device type, and anonymous usage signals may be used to choose which ad to show
  • In the EEA, UK and similar regions, Google's consent form is shown before personalized ads are requested
  • Premium and trial users never see ads, and the ad SDK is not initialised for them
  • Google processes this data according to their Privacy Policy and Advertising Policy

4.6 Expo Platform

BattleCall is built using Expo/React Native framework. Expo may collect anonymous analytics data about app crashes and performance. This data does not contain any personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

All your personal data (alarms, goals, recordings, object-scan photos, preferences) is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms:

  • AsyncStorage: For app settings and user preferences
  • SharedPreferences: For alarm configurations (Android)
  • File System: For audio recordings, custom media, and object-scan photos

5.2 Data Backup

Your app data may be included in Android's automatic backup system. You can disable this in your device's backup settings if desired.

5.3 Security Measures

  • Data is stored locally and protected by your device's security (PIN, pattern, biometrics)
  • maken.dev operates no servers of its own. Data leaves your device only for Google Gemini (AI Coach), RevenueCat (purchase verification), Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage), and Google AdMob (ads for free users), always over HTTPS
  • Audio recordings are stored locally and never uploaded without your explicit action
  • QR code data is generated and stored locally on your device
  • Object-scan photos are stored locally and used only to match the same object when you dismiss an alarm

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT share, sell, or disclose your personal information to third parties, except:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly choose to export or share data (e.g., sharing a QR code)
  • For AI Features: When you use AI Coach, data is sent to Google Gemini API as described above
  • For Purchases: An anonymous user ID and purchase receipt are shared with RevenueCat to verify subscription entitlements
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Analytics & Ads: Anonymous usage events and your resettable advertising ID are sent to Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob, as described in sections 4.2 and 4.5
  • App Store Analytics: Anonymous usage data may be shared with Google Play Store for app improvement
✅ No Data Selling: We will NEVER sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties. Your resettable Android advertising ID is used to measure our campaigns and, for free users, to serve AdMob ads. Premium removes all ads.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View Data: All your data is visible within the app
  • Edit Data: Modify or delete alarms, goals, and preferences at any time
  • Export Data: Export QR codes and recordings you've created
  • Delete Data: Use the "Reset All Data" option in settings to erase all app data

7.2 Opt-Out Options

  • AI Features: Choose traditional ringtones instead of AI Coach to avoid data transmission
  • Permissions: Revoke any permission in your device settings (may limit functionality)
  • Ad Tracking: Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Ads: Upgrade to Premium to remove all in-app ads
  • Review Prompts: Choose "Don't ask again" and BattleCall will never ask you to rate it again

7.3 Data Deletion

To completely delete your data:

  1. Open BattleCall and go to Settings
  2. Tap "Reset All Data" to erase all app data
  3. Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored files

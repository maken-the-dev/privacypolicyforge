Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Forge handles information when you use the Android app. Habit data stays on your device. Some information is processed by Google Play, Google advertising services, and RevenueCat so subscriptions and ads can work.

Effective: 11 November 2026 App: Forge (com.maken.dev.forge) Operator: Maken.dev, a trader

1. Who we are

Forge is provided by Maken.dev (“we”, “us”), a trader acting for purposes relating to trade, business, craft, or profession. Consumer protection laws in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom apply to this app.

We do not run a Forge user-account server. Your name, birthday, profile photo, habits, completions, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device unless a third-party service below needs a limited subset of technical data.

2. Information we collect

Stored on your device

  • Profile. Display name, optional birthday, and optional profile photo (camera or photo library).
  • Habits and progress. Habit names, times, locations, notes, timers, counters, subtasks, completions, streaks, XP, levels, and trophies.
  • Preferences. Theme, notification mode, and similar settings.

Processed by third parties when the app is used

  • Advertising identifier and ad events when ads are shown (free version).
  • Purchase and entitlement data if you subscribe to Forge Pro (Google Play Billing via RevenueCat).
  • Device and diagnostics data typical of Android, Google Play, and ad SDKs (device type, OS version, app version, IP address, crash or request logs).

We do not ask for your email inside the app. If you email us, we will see the address and content you send.

3. How we use information

  • To run Forge: reminders, streaks, stats, themes, and the Flow / List / Routine / Stats experience.
  • To remember that you completed onboarding and whether Forge Pro is active.
  • To show ads if you are not subscribed to Forge Pro.
  • To process, restore, and manage subscriptions.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.
We do not sell your habit list, name, birthday, or profile photo. Those stay on your device unless you choose to share a screenshot or progress image yourself.

4. Advertising

The free version of Forge uses Google AdMob (rewarded interstitial ads). Ads may use the Android Advertising ID and may be personalized unless you opt out in your device advertising settings or through any consent choices presented to you.

Forge Pro removes ads. See Google’s policy: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Forge Pro is billed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Pro entitlement is active and to restore purchases. RevenueCat and Google process purchase identifiers and status. They do not need your habit content.

Current advertised prices: monthly USD 4.99 and yearly USD 49.99. The price shown in Google Play at checkout is the price you pay, including applicable tax. Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled in Google Play.

7. Third-party processors

  • Google Play / Google LLC — distribution, billing, ads
  • RevenueCat — subscription status

Their own policies apply to data they process as independent controllers or processors. If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases include performing the contract (providing the app you requested), legitimate interests (keeping the free app sustainable through ads, security), consent where required for advertising or optional photos, and legal obligation.

8. Retention and security

Local data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall Forge. Purchase records are kept by Google and RevenueCat according to their retention rules. No method of storage is 100% secure; we limit what leaves the device.

9. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use contractual safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because most Forge content is on-device, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling. For ads and purchases, use Google Play / device settings, or email us.

EEA/UK: you may complain to your local data protection authority. California: we do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are commonly used in the CCPA, except that advertising SDKs may use identifiers for cross-context advertising unless you opt out.

11. Children

Forge is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age of digital consent in your country, which may be up to 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided information, contact us and we will help you delete on-device data and any related support emails.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy. Material changes that require consent will be handled as required by law.
